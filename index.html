Research Assistant · LUMS

Imran Haider

Human-centered AI, religion, and the human factors of software engineering

I am a Research Assistant in the Software Engineering for Good (SFG) Lab at the Lahore University of Management Sciences, working with Dr. Abdul Ali Bangash. I study how AI systems should be shaped by the people and communities they affect. My current work asks how religious traditions are represented in AI, how such systems can respect principled disagreement among scholars, and what role religious authorities should play in deciding how their faith is encoded in algorithms. I also study how developers work with AI coding tools and who carries the cost of checking their output. I hold a Bachelor of Electrical Engineering from NUST and am studying Dars-e-Nizami at Al Burhan Institute.
